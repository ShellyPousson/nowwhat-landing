Where to find downloaded files on Android

By Now What? · Updated November 10, 2026

Begin with the app you used to download the file. A file that was saved from a browser and an item saved inside an app may appear in different places.

If you downloaded it in Chrome

  1. Open Chrome on your phone.
  2. Open the menu marked with three dots, then choose Downloads.
  3. If Chrome's address bar is at the bottom, you may instead need to swipe up on that bar to find Downloads.
  4. Find the item you intended to save and try opening it.

Look in the phone's file manager

Open your phone's Files app and look for downloaded items. The app name and layout can vary by manufacturer. If you remember the filename, use the file manager's search when available.

What if I used another app?

Return to the app where you selected Download or Save. Look for its saved items or downloads area. Write down the app name and the name of the item before asking for help; that is more useful than searching random folders.

Is it missing, or will it not open?

These are different problems. If the filename is visible but tapping it produces an error, record that message. If the download is still in progress or failed, address that first. Finding a filename does not confirm that a complete, readable file was saved.

Check the outcome

Open the intended file and confirm its contents are what you expected. Avoid deleting files while you are still trying to locate the right one.

Reference: Google Chrome Help: downloads on Android. These general instructions may differ on your phone.
