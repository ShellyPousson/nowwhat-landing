Privacy & data

Data & account deletion

Now What? currently does not maintain a separate server-side Now What? user-profile database. Android sign-in authenticates access to the protected service; it does not create a separate profile record in a Now What? database. If you want to request deletion of a Now What?-related account identity or associated information anyway, the request path below is available to you.

Delete task screenshots and local workflow data

  1. Open Now What? on your Android device.
  2. Tap Start over to clear the active saved task and its locally stored screenshots.
  3. To remove all local Now What? app data, use Android's app settings to clear storage/data, or uninstall the app.

Remove local sign-in state

Tap Sign out in Now What? to clear the app's locally stored authentication state. If your login is provided by another identity provider, deleting that provider account must be done with that provider.

Request data or account deletion from Canais

If you want Canais LLC to review and delete a Now What?-related account identity or information under its control that may be associated with your use of Now What?, email support@nowwhat.it.com with the subject Now What? data deletion request.

Include only enough information to identify the request. Do not send passwords or screenshots containing sensitive information. Canais may need to verify that a request relates to you before acting on it.

What this request does not automatically delete

Deleting Now What? local data or asking Canais for deletion does not automatically delete an account you hold with an independent identity provider. Information already processed by infrastructure or AI service providers may also be subject to their applicable retention periods and legal obligations.

For more detail, read the Now What? Privacy Policy.
